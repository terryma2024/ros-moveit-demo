# Head Search feedback freshness and scheduling amendment

Status: proposed for independent GPT-6 Astra / High review. No production policy or calibration value has changed. This amendment follows the approved Head Search design, runtime revision 4, and the reservation-binding amendment. It covers Head Search on antzb only.

## Observed failures

HS331 ran the installed production smoke with the repaired native controller. The stack published the head camera at 10 Hz and loaded the selected detector on CUDA. Seven neck goals were accepted and then canceled. Search timed out without a lock or completed image inference. The sealed journal counted 25 controller ticks as consumed images, although they contained only seven distinct source stamps. The independent aggregate refused search, lock and cadence. The failed run remains retained.

The adapter polls every 2 ms. Between 100 ms camera exposures it treats the absence of a newly fresh image as INPUT_STALE and cancels the current bounded neck goal. A goal can therefore be admitted repeatedly without completing the motion needed to start image inference. Camera admission age, stream liveness and joint freshness need separate definitions.

HS332 and HS333 used the same installed composition, camera subscriptions and five serial 20 s joint-reader profiles. Changing the executor from four workers to one spinning thread restored approximately 100 Hz delivery even with the original BEST_EFFORT KEEP_LAST(5) profile. The original profile delivered 1124 samples under four workers and 2000 under one thread. Deeper queues under four workers sometimes retained contiguous source stamps while delivering them too late to establish freshness. They do not establish a safe fix by themselves.

The raw measurements retain source simulation stamps, receipt monotonic stamps, callback simulation clock readings and decision clock readings separately. `head-reservation-runtime-20261002T021647Z/feedback-qos-ab-summary.json` records every raw-file hash and an explicit linear quantile definition. These are diagnostic windows, not final calibration distributions. The selected depth-five profile records 34 negative callback source ages and zero negative decision source ages. All negative observations remain recorded and must not be clipped to zero. The diagnostic read-before-snapshot ordering is not the final decision sampling rule.

HS331 also exposed evidence gaps: reservation ACK and action results were not linked by UUID in the journal; stop normalization discarded the collected timestamps; a claimed controller stop was inferred from an adapter field instead of calling the real port. Those gaps must be closed before qualification.

## Executor and subscriptions

Use one spinning SingleThreadedExecutor for the Head Search sensor/action node. Detector inference remains one separately bounded job. The control/watchdog loop remains separate from the executor so it can check deadlines and initiate cancellation without waiting for another camera exposure. Callbacks must not block on action or service futures.

Keep the existing camera QoS. Retain the original depth-five joint QoS unless the formal workload measurement disproves its delivery under the chosen executor. Do not use deep history to refresh an old source stamp. Record the actual executor type, callback-group identities, reader QoS, publisher QoS and delivered source periods in the calibration dependencies and runtime readback.

Remove duplicate forwarding of a joint callback to a port that already owns a subscription. Construct the reserved controller port once and inject it into the neck port; do not create and abandon an earlier controller subscription when replacing the port. Existing unrelated ACT consumers keep their existing interfaces and behavior. The Head Search v2 path requires the new explicit policy binding.

## Clock and policy contract

Keep the 17 calibration value names and units. `max_age_s` retains its end-to-end camera freshness definition: decision monotonic time minus the original image receipt monotonic time, checked both at admission and at inference completion before any result is used. It is not a joint bound or a camera interarrival period. CameraInfo and causal frame/joint pairing retain their identity and `max_skew_s` checks.

Add a separately sealed `feedback-policy.json` to the fresh calibration bundle. Its schema declares the consumers and clocks and binds these measured quantities:

- `joint_max_receipt_age_s`: decision monotonic time minus the original joint receipt monotonic time.
- `joint_max_source_age_s`: current node simulation time minus the original joint source simulation stamp.
- `camera_interarrival_deadline_s`: elapsed monotonic time since the latest valid, advancing camera receipt, checked by the stream watchdog.

The policy also binds the measured joint producer period, selected reader QoS/executor, measurement documents and formula identifiers. Use the clock-specific formulas and acceptance rules below; no simulation period is added to a monotonic age. Report p50/p95/p99/max, sample counts, source gaps, negative ages and the margin separately. The diagnostic values are not promoted values and provide no fallback defaults.

Freeze the provisional calibration-only inputs at the entry precondition described below, before any motion. Independently verify and seal the final distributions in the installed formal timing phase under the real camera, detector, neck and stop consumers, after startup. The existing phase order remains non-resumable. Save the new policy measurement document as an additional timing-phase artifact and include its hash in the bundle. If the required measurement is absent, mixed across identities, outside an existing hard safety bound or unsuitable for the declared workload, refuse the bundle and report the reason. Do not fit this policy to held-out scenario outcomes.

The exact human bundle approval must cover all 17 values plus the policy values, formulas, margins and dependency hashes. Neither e218 nor pending0563 approves this policy. Promotion, the final installed configuration and every qualifier must bind the approved bundle and policy hashes. Read back the 17 existing values and the additional policy consumers separately; a 17-of-17 match alone cannot qualify the new policy. A modified executor, QoS or policy invalidates the old bundle and runtime receipts.

## Frame admission and bounded motion

Admit each advancing physical image at most once per attempt, including images observed while the neck is moving. Record frame admission separately from control/watchdog polls and inference completion. Duplicate, backward, pre-reset and pre-motion images never increase cadence or lock counts.

An interval between expected camera exposures does not submit another neck goal and does not cancel an already admitted bounded goal solely because no new image exists. During that interval, retain the current bounded goal only while the continuous joint receipt/source freshness checks, ownership/generation guard, camera stream watchdog and total search deadline all hold. On any violation, request stop and require actual stop confirmation. A missing camera beyond its bound, future/backward joint state, unavailable clock, lease loss or detector failure remains fail-closed.

Only a newly admitted fresh image and its causal joint sample can produce a new target or enter image inference. Keep the existing safe interval, command guard, cumulative coarse/fine limits, confidence/geometry checks and three-frame lock rule. Inference results retain their original frame identity and receipt time; an overdue or reset-generation result cannot create a target or lock. Pending inference has a bounded watchdog and cannot conceal stale camera or joint feedback.

The search deadline remains monotonic and cumulative across motion, waits and inference. This amendment does not increase `search_timeout_s`, inherit another consumer's budget or relabel timeout as a successful negative outcome. Formal calibration must measure the final scheduling behavior; an unsatisfied held-out gate remains a refusal.

## Stop and journal contract

For Head Search, stop confirmation requires terminal/rejected action state for every dispatched goal, the actual controller port's stop predicate, and at least three consecutive distinct advancing joint samples collected after the current stop request, within the explicit receipt/source bounds, stop deadline and velocity limit. Preserve original source and receipt stamps, the decision clocks, velocities, goal states and cancellation acknowledgments in the sealed journal. Re-reading one sample cannot count three times. Future, backward, duplicate or stale samples reset/refuse the proof rather than becoming quiet feedback.

Link each reservation to the exact goal UUID, typed-goal description, payload SHA256, owner generation, controller incarnation/boot, ACK time, dispatch time, action acceptance and terminal or cancellation result. Preserve the original production claim window and all fail-closed admission rules. Export records from the real reserved port instead of inferring admission from an intended target.

The disk-reading aggregators must recompute cadence from distinct admitted image identities, verify inference-to-frame linkage, reconstruct action/stop evidence and validate the approved policy binding. They must reject missing or contradictory raw evidence. Preserve the original HS331 journal and its current PASS/FAIL results; strengthened gates do not rewrite that historical verdict.

## Normative bootstrap and numerical rules (revision 2)

These rules resolve the first independent review. They govern the statements above wherever a shorter description leaves behavior unspecified.

Before the existing trajectory precondition or any of the eight phases can move the neck, the installed entry performs a calibration-only, stopped bootstrap on one owned composition. It validates the unchanged C2a weights, geometry and candidate identities, actual 100 Hz joint publisher, 10 Hz head publisher, single executor and depth-five readers. The actual native ports, camera/CameraInfo/TF and selected CUDA detector are constructed once. No native motion goal is sent. After discovery, CUDA warmup and ten seconds of stable advancing streams, collect one complete 20-monotonic-second window with the actual subscribers and one real inference per advancing settled image. Observe the actual watchdog concurrently. There is no synthetic GIL load or detector-free substitute in this bootstrap.

The bootstrap is owned by the brand-new formal run root and must succeed before any motion. A diagnostic entry uses a separate fresh root and the same bootstrap. Freeze its input references, raw measurements, formulas and provisional values in a regular `calibration-only-policy.json` with `purpose=calibration`, exact runtime/model/geometry/executor/QoS hashes and the whole document's digest. The seed 17 values are the byte-verified historical e218 bundle as reference measurements, explicitly unapproved for this runtime. Recompute provisional camera age/skew from the fresh bootstrap using the existing phase-7 formulas and margins. Other seed values remain exactly recorded. The bootstrap does not approve or promote any seed or measured value. Its identity is included in every subsequent calibration motion receipt.

Only the installed formal `run` entry and an explicitly diagnostic smoke entry may consume a calibration-only policy. Normal smoke, scenario, restart, readback and Teleop qualifiers require an exact human-approved bundle/policy binding and reject `purpose=calibration` regardless of any user-editable `approved` boolean. A diagnostic journal carries `diagnostic_only=true`; the final aggregator refuses that journal as qualification evidence even when its functional checks pass. Thus a provisional input cannot become a production fallback. The eight phases retain their existing order and are never resumed. A bootstrap or later freshness failure terminates the run; no bound is enlarged in place. Phase 7 independently measures the final moving/stopping workload and refuses any observed violation of the frozen provisional bounds. It seals the recomputed final policy only if the acceptance rules hold. A new attempt starts at a fresh root and entry bootstrap.

Decision sampling occurs at a declared nominal 5 ms monotonic interval. Under one short cache lock, first copy the complete original feedback snapshot and its epoch/source/receipt stamps; release the lock, then read monotonic-before, node-simulation-now and monotonic-after. Receipt age uses monotonic-after. Source age uses node-simulation-now. Retain all three decision clocks and the copied sample identity. A callback's earlier simulation-clock reading is diagnostic metadata, not the decision clock. Never read decision clocks and then select a newer sample. The same rule applies to camera source-age decisions.

The full window must contain at least 3900 scheduled decision snapshots, 1900 original joint messages and 190 advancing camera messages, and at least 19 simulation seconds of coverage. Keep every sample, gap, missed poll and invalid observation. Validate the actual source periods against the declared 100/10 Hz publishers, with a 1e-6 s numerical stamp tolerance, and require no internal missing or backward source interval. Source-period quantiles use simulation stamps. Wall-period quantiles use original receipt stamps and are independent measurements; no real-time-factor conversion is used. Report real-time factor without assuming it equals one.

For every series, quantiles use linear interpolation at `(n-1)*p`; all finite decision ages and all original advancing receipt gaps are included. A negative or missing decision age, clock regression, zero-progress clock, or nonfinite/missing sample refuses that window. Negative callback metadata remains recorded and is not relabeled sensor corruption merely because the asynchronous node clock had not caught up. Observations above a derived bound refuse the window; no clipping, outlier deletion, substitution of a maximum or repeat-until-pass selection is allowed.

Let Js be joint source-period p50 in simulation seconds, Jm be joint receipt-period p50 in monotonic seconds, Cs be camera source-period p50 in simulation seconds, and Cm be camera receipt-period p50 in monotonic seconds. The separate formulas are:

- joint receipt bound = decision receipt-age p99 + Jm, in monotonic seconds;
- joint source bound = decision source-age p99 + Js, in simulation seconds;
- camera receipt-gap watchdog bound = advancing receipt-gap p99 + Jm, in monotonic seconds;
- camera source-age bound = decision source-age p99 + Cs, in simulation seconds;
- clock receipt-gap bound = original advancing `/clock` receipt-gap p99 + Jm, in monotonic seconds.

Each formula, quantile, period, sample population and margin is recorded. Additional source-age and clock-watchdog fields are sealed policy quantities beyond the first three quantities listed above. Source bounds may not exceed two respective source periods. Camera gap bound may not exceed two camera monotonic periods. Joint and clock receipt bounds must be strictly below `stop_latency_s - watchdog_reaction_limit_s`. All these comparisons use the stated matching clock units. The existing camera max-age and skew ceilings still apply to the 17-value formulas. If a required ceiling cannot be satisfied, refuse rather than clamp.

The proposed watchdog nominal period is 0.002 monotonic seconds, matching the existing poll interval. Its proposed hard reaction limit is 0.010 monotonic seconds. These are newly explicit policy quantities, not inherited camera/joint defaults or already approved numerical values. Bootstrap and phase 7 must measure every interval and fault-to-cancellation interval and refuse any interval above the limit. The exact human bundle approval must include both quantities and all formula ceilings. The existing stop latency remains unchanged during diagnostics and must be remeasured by the formal phase; stop confirmation has to fit inside that value including any watchdog delay.

## Normative frame and inference state (revision 2)

Each attempt has an immutable generation, reset source floor, admitted-image watermark, inference job token, motion epoch and stream watchdog state. Reset cancels the old operation, requests a proven stop, clears the watermarks/queues and creates a new generation. No motion is allowed before the first valid camera/CameraInfo pair, valid current joint decision snapshot, advancing clock and ownership guard. The first-image startup limit is the frozen camera gap bound measured from attempt activation; exceeding it requests stop and refuses the attempt.

A camera callback only queues a candidate with its original source/receipt stamps and payload identity. A candidate refreshes the stream watchdog only after shape/encoding/frame identity, epoch/source-floor, strict source progression, original receipt age, camera source age and exact matching CameraInfo checks pass. Refresh stores the original receipt time, not the later polling time. A duplicate, backward, delayed buffered, pre-reset or invalid candidate cannot refresh it. If a temporarily future source stamp becomes clock-valid later, it still uses the original receipt time; it receives no extra age budget. Invalid candidates and reasons are recorded.

The pending candidate queue has capacity one. A new source can replace an unadmitted candidate, but the displaced identity and reason are recorded as a dropped exposure. An admitted candidate advances the admission watermark exactly once. Motion-time exposures enter the cadence record as distinct admitted images with `inference_skipped=unsettled`; they never become settled observations later. A new settled observation must be strictly after the motion epoch's terminal/settled source floor, satisfy actual position and velocity tolerances, and pass the causal joint/CameraInfo/TF checks. Cadence uses admitted exposure identities; detector/lock evidence uses separately linked completed settled observations. Queue drops, skipped inference and watchdog polls cannot be silently counted as successful inference or extra frames.

There is at most one inference job. Its immutable record binds generation, motion epoch, frame/CameraInfo source and original receipt stamps, joint snapshot, token and dispatch time. Its deadline is the earlier of original image receipt plus frozen `max_age_s`, original CameraInfo receipt plus the same bound, and the cumulative search deadline. The worker's completion is only a candidate result. Before accepting it, recheck the completion age against both original receipt times, token/generation/motion epoch, ownership, current joint source/receipt freshness, source causality, settled state and post-motion floor. A stale or reset result cannot make a command or increase lock count. An expired job requests stop and refuses; a late completion is retained as discarded evidence. No new inference overtakes it, and it cannot postpone stream/watchdog checks or the original cumulative timeout.

## Normative stop epoch and watchdog enforcement (revision 2)

A stop request records its unique stop epoch, attempt/generation, monotonic request time, simulation request stamp, fault time where applicable and the last admitted sample identities. Admission closes before cancellation requests are queued. Unknown acceptance or missing terminal state remains unconfirmed; cancellation ACK alone is insufficient. Actual accepted goals must reach terminal state, and every goal state is linked to its reservation/UUID.

The quiet run starts at zero. Only original samples whose receipt and source are strictly after the stop request's matching-clock stamps and whose source/receipt identities strictly advance can enter it. Evaluate samples in original observed receipt order and reject a source regression; never sort raw samples to manufacture progression. A moving, stale, future, regressed, duplicate, nonfinite or wrong-epoch sample resets/refuses the quiet run; samples before the request cannot enter it. Require three consecutive qualifying samples, actual port.stopped() true and all goal terminal/rejection states confirmed. The final predicate observation has its own monotonic/source clocks and must occur no later than request time plus the unchanged `stop_latency_s`. For a watchdog fault with a known crossing time, the same deadline starts at that first threshold crossing; reaction delay is included, not added afterward. A missed deadline remains a refusal even if the stack later becomes quiet.

The watchdog checks frozen clock/camera/joint freshness, ownership/generation, executor progress, inference expiry and cumulative search deadline on its own scheduled loop. Reservation exchange, service calls, inference, logging and stop confirmation never run synchronously on that loop or while it holds a cache/operation lock. Dispatch/cancel work uses a separate bounded operation queue. Each operation retains the epoch and must revalidate admission before sending; a revoked/expired queued operation cannot send a later goal. Native claim/deadline/UUID rules remain authoritative. Stop confirmation is observed asynchronously so the watchdog can continue checking its own progress.

An executor, `/clock` or watchdog progress gap beyond its bound closes admission and initiates cancellation. Add one owned CPU-only monitor process within the same composition, started by exec before CUDA loads. It has no detector or MuJoCo instance. It independently checks the watchdog heartbeat with the same hard reaction bound and uses the neck action cancellation service for the recorded current-epoch UUIDs if the watchdog stalls. It does not send motion goals, use broad process signals or target unrelated controllers. Record its PID/start identity, node name, policy hash and action-cancel receipts; the graph whitelist admits exactly this owned monitor and refuses a foreign or duplicate one. The existing stack owner starts and tears it down with the other owned children. Its own monotonic/node clock and original joint receipts must establish the same stop proof; failure to do so is a refusal, not a claimed stop. A stalled Python executor or inference worker cannot be allowed to defeat both monitors by holding a common lock. Tests must stall each component, delay reservation I/O and make callbacks hold the GIL; they must demonstrate actual cancellation initiation and confirmation within the preserved stop deadline. If this architecture cannot meet those timings, reject the runtime and redesign rather than enlarging the limits. The aggregator reconstructs epochs, ordered raw sample runs, predicate observation, fault/request deadlines and the action chain from the sealed file and approved policy hashes.


## Normative monitor authority and recovery protocol (revision 3)

The independent monitor is the fail-closed authority for one attempt's neck generation. At owned startup it receives the exact generation, controller incarnation/boot, simulation session, role-scoped native capability and endpoint, primary PID/start identity, immutable attempt epoch and policy hash through authenticated private IPC. Both sides validate peer UID/PID/start identity and the epoch. Capabilities are never printed in receipts. Startup has separate presence, armed-generation and dispatch-readiness stages as specified below. Reservation and dispatch require independent native identity agreement and a recorded authenticated readiness ACK; arming occurs earlier without motion authority. The monitor cannot arm a newer generation or issue a motion goal.

Before any native reservation request, the operation worker registers the immutable goal UUID, typed-goal/payload digest, owner generation, controller identity, epoch, operation sequence and reservation claim/deadline with the monitor. The monitor retains the inventory record and acknowledges its exact digest/epoch/sequence. Reservation and action dispatch are prohibited until that ACK arrives. This inventory includes every prepared, reserved, in-flight, acceptance-pending, accepted and terminal operation; cancellation inventory is never limited to already accepted goals. A failed or mismatched registration closes admission and requests stop. Native binding and claim windows do not change.

On a primary fault, the monitor atomically marks the epoch REVOKED, records the fault-crossing time and its own stop-request epoch/clocks, and independently sends the authenticated SOGR generation-close operation to the scoped native neck endpoint. It validates the close reply's protocol, ACK status and exact generation against the frozen identity. This requires a strict close API; the current failure-suppressing ReservationAuthority.close() is insufficient and remains unsuitable for this safety proof. Record request/reply identities and timestamps without secret capability bytes. No local cancel flag or cancellation ACK substitutes for native close ACK.

After validated native closure, all later admissions in that generation must be refused. The monitor also cancels every inventoried UUID, including unknown/pending acceptance. It independently reconciles native action status/result and cancellation-service replies. A first unknown-goal reply is not completion: an action admitted just before closure can become visible afterward. Continue cancellation/result reconciliation until each inventoried operation is proven rejected-before-admission or reaches a terminal state, and the three post-request quiet samples and actual port predicate establish stop within the unchanged fault-to-stop deadline. Already terminal operations retain their terminal proof. Pending or unknown acceptance at the deadline refuses stop even if current velocities are quiet.

A lost, malformed or mismatched native close ACK is unconfirmed revocation. Continue scoped cancellation and record the failed closure; do not claim safety equivalence or reopen the epoch. If authoritative closure/stop cannot be established by the preserved deadline, the monitor directs the existing owner to retire this exact owned simulator/controller composition using recorded PID/start identities, without broad signals or touching another stack. Report an unconfirmed stop/failed run even if teardown later clears every process. Final aggregation requires the validated native close ACK and timely stop evidence, so forced teardown cannot turn that failure into qualification.

Primary and monitor have mutually checked monotonic heartbeats and authenticated IPC sequence numbers. A dead/stale monitor, broken channel or mismatched epoch closes primary admission before any further reserve or dispatch; the primary requests the same strict native closure and cancellation/stop sequence. Each operation worker revalidates the monitor's current epoch and liveness both before reservation and immediately before action send. Recovery from any stall consumes the monitor's authoritative REVOKED state; old queued operations and stale heartbeat messages cannot clear it or arm/reserve a replacement generation. Neither process may re-open a faulted attempt. A new generation requires a new non-resumable attempt/root and fresh owned controller incarnation after verified teardown.

Each process records its own observed fault crossing. When the primary is unresponsive, the monitor owns the crossing timestamp, stop epoch, raw feedback and decision clocks, strict-close/cancel/status/result collection and the actual stop predicate. It uses its own bounded ROS clients/subscriptions and independent cache; it does not wait for a primary response, shared primary lock or primary logger. The earlier observable fault time across the two processes sets the deadline where both records exist. The monitor's port includes every inventoried operation before it can report stopped. Journal aggregation reads both original sealed streams and rejects missing identity, inventory ACK, close ACK, terminal reconciliation, heartbeat progression or stop timing evidence.

Discriminating native/runtime controls must reserve-ACK then delay action send until revocation; cancel a registered UUID before server acceptance; race acceptance with strict native closure; lose a close ACK; terminate the monitor; and recover a stalled primary with queued operations. Every control must prove no later motion admission in the revoked generation, no stale-epoch revival and the preserved fault-to-stop deadline, or retain an explicit failed/unconfirmed run. These controls complement the already approved digest/tamper/replay rules; they do not replace them.


## Native provisioning and startup boundary (revision 4)

The existing main reservation endpoint retains its exact primary UID/PID/start-ticks check, capability, roles, digest/goal/generation rules and 50 ms connection deadline. Do not add a second PID to that endpoint or weaken its peer check.

Add one optional Head Search neck revocation endpoint in the same native controller process, sharing the same gate. It has a separate random capability and exactly one separately provisioned monitor UID/PID/start-ticks identity. The private provision binds the monitor, native owner PID/start identity, simulation session, neck role and short socket path. Only an identity query and generation-close operation are permitted at this endpoint. Arming, reservation, ingress authorization and action goals are forbidden there, even with its valid capability. The controller rejects foreign/reused-PID peers, wrong capability/role/session, stale generation, unexpected opcodes and symlinked/occupied/overlong paths. Provisioning is required for the Head Search v2 composition and absent for unrelated legacy consumers. Record both endpoint identities and provision hashes without secrets.

The revocation endpoint has its own service thread, independent listener and bounded frame parsing; it never waits for the main reservation worker or its incomplete frame. Its proposed connection deadline is 2 ms, an additional explicit policy quantity subject to runtime verification and exact human bundle approval. Verify gate-lock acquisition, close processing and cancellation initiation together against the 10 ms reaction limit. Keep wire decoding and any potentially large allocations/comparisons out of the critical revocation path. A slow/incomplete main connection cannot block the revocation listener. Cancellation is initiated concurrently with strict native close, not deferred until its ACK; the final proof still requires both validated native closure and terminal reconciliation. A stuck close or ACK causes an unconfirmed safety failure and owned teardown, not an increased timeout or a successful proof.

Monitor-authorized fault closure is irreversible for that controller incarnation: it latches fault revocation and refuses every later arm/reserve/admit until verified owned controller teardown and a fresh incarnation. Regular primary normal-terminal closure retains the existing sequential-generation behavior; it does not clear a monitor fault latch. Lifecycle cleanup/reconfigure and recovered queued operations cannot clear the fault latch in place. This strengthens the fault path without replacing regular main-peer authorization or the existing tombstones.

Startup ordering is explicit:

1. Start the CPU-only monitor by exec and record its PID/start identity. Create separate primary and monitor provisions, then start the one native composition default-closed. The monitor acknowledges authenticated presence, matching scoped provisioning and liveness, without requiring a native armed identity yet. No motion is permitted.
2. Complete the stopped bootstrap and freeze its provisional policy. The monitor acknowledges that exact policy/epoch/hash. Native ownership, active controllers, quiet feedback and both peers are verified. Failed bootstrap/presence/policy ACK retires the owned stack without arming or motion.
3. The primary may arm the one declared generation through its unchanged main endpoint. This stage allows no reservation, inventory dispatch or action. It reads the now-available SOIA identity. The monitor independently reads SOIA through its close-only endpoint and verifies the same generation/incarnation/boot and owned native PID/start identity.
4. Only the monitor's authenticated ARMED_READY ACK, bound to that identity, policy and epoch, opens UUID-inventory registration. Each operation still needs its own inventory ACK before reservation or dispatch. Any failure in the armed-but-not-ready interval triggers independent strict close and teardown. A readiness ACK never grants a newer generation or clears revocation.

The monitor validates native close replies against the exact owner process/channel and armed identity; retries use the same epoch/generation and never arm. A foreign or stale response cannot qualify as an ACK. The native identity interface remains unchanged: identity is unavailable while default-closed, and startup does not query it as if arming had already occurred.

Add actual native controls with the two real peer processes: the primary endpoint must reject the monitor; the close-only endpoint must reject arm/reserve/foreign/reused-PID requests; correct monitor close must revoke the shared gate and keep it revoked across attempted lifecycle/arm recovery; stale close cannot revoke another generation. Hold the primary connection genuinely incomplete for its 50 ms parsing deadline, then prove the independent listener closes and cancellation starts inside the proposed reaction limit. Verify lost/malformed ACK and acceptance-race cases retain failed/unconfirmed results where proof is missing. All endpoint deadlines, identities and lifecycle outcomes become sealed runtime dependencies and are independently read back.
